Ancient history

[edit]
  • The Pyramids of Egypt were not constructed with slave labor.[2] Archaeological evidence shows that the laborers were skilled workers and poor farmers. The idea that slaves were used originated with Herodotus.
  • Tutankhamun's tomb is not inscribed with a curse. This was a media invention of 20th-century U.S. tabloid journalists.[citation needed]
